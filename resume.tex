\section{Professional Summary}

Software engineer focused on end-to-end systems, 0-to-1 product and technical execution, and simplifying complex workflows. Currently building full-stack financial systems at Ellipsis Labs; previously early engineer/sales engineering lead at D2iQ (Mesosphere), founder, and data infrastructure engineer at Airbnb and Citadel, with earlier work in telecom and instrumentation. Author of two Rust books, creator of open-source projects (10,000+ GitHub stars), and contributor to LLM agent and Rust ML tooling.
