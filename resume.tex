\section{Professional Summary}

Software engineer focused on infrastructure, distributed systems, and developer tooling, primarily in Rust. Currently building financial infrastructure at Ellipsis Labs; previously worked on data and infrastructure systems at Airbnb, Citadel, Braze, and D2iQ. Author of two Rust books and creator of open-source projects with more than 10,000 GitHub stars. Focused on correctness, operational simplicity, and systems that remain easy to understand as they grow.
